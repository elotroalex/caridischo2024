blurb_en: The Waves Ahead Digital Archive aims to provide access to the history of
  Waves Ahead Puerto Rico and highlight its efforts to provide psychological services
  and promote education, prevention, and public policy in support of older adults
  in the LGBTQ+ community. The digital repository brings together materials such as
  photographs, promotional materials, awards, and other digital/digitized documentation
  of the various initiatives that the community-based organization has developed since
  its founding in 2017. This Digital Humanities project was developed by participants
  of the 2022-2023 cohort of the DH Scholars in Residence program of the UPR Caribe
  Digital Project, with support from the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation (https://uprcaribedigital.org/colaboradores/).

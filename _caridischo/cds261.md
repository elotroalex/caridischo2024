blurb_en: 'Puerto Rico’s Bosque Modelo Nacional is a territorial management proposal
  created through Law 182-2014. This management approach focuses on partnerships with
  a shared vision for the sustainable development of a vast landscape where diverse
  land uses, activities, and values ​​coexist. This digital exhibit is a series of
  eight interactive maps showing the territories protected by the Bosque Modelo de
  Puerto Rico group. The maps highlight key organizations in the ecological zone,
  highlighting their contributions to environmental management and conservation, as
  well as the ecological and historical-cultural values ​​of the area. The exhibit
  is intended as a powerful educational tool that seeks to raise awareness about sustainability
  and foster a sense of shared responsibility for preserving the natural environment.
  Through the combination of technology and storytelling, this project offers an innovative
  way to connect people with the Bosque Modelo and its conservation efforts: "en el
  Bosque Modelo todos somos vecinos y vecinas" (“in the Bosque Modelo, we are all
  neighbors”). This Digital Humanities project was developed by participants of the
  2023-2024 cohort of the DH Scholars in Residence program of the UPR Caribe Digital
  Project, with support from the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation (https://uprcaribedigital.org/colaboradores/).'

  se propone como una herramienta educativa poderosa que busca aumentar la conciencia

  la preservación del entorno natural. A través de la combinación de tecnología y
  narración, este proyecto ofrece una manera innovadora de conectar a las personas

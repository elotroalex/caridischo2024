  por la independencia de Puerto Rico a actuales y futuras generaciones, y garantizar
  la continuidad de su legado mediante un repositorio digital accesible, a pesar de

blurb_en: The Juan Mari Brás Digital Archive preserves historical documents of this
  Puerto Rican independence leader, focusing on his personal and political life. The
  Archive seeks to make his thinking, work, and history of struggle for Puerto Rican
  independence known to current and future generations, and to guarantee the continuity
  of his legacy through an accessible digital repository, despite the challenges of
  ensuring the physical preservation of the materials. The archive houses the exhibit
  “Las conexiones de Juan Mari Brás," an interactive map that highlights the correspondence
  maintained by Puerto Rican independence leader Juan Mari Brás from 1969 to 1997,
  on various topics (but with a marked emphasis on politics), highlighting Mari Brás's
  connections with Latin America and other parts of the world. This Digital Humanities
  project was developed by participants of the 2022-2023 cohort of the DH Scholars
  in Residence program of the UPR Caribe Digital Project, with support from the Andrew
  W. Mellon Foundation (https://uprcaribedigital.org/colaboradores/).

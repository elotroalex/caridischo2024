blurb_en: 'Somos Cangrejos: Mapa, Arte, Foro is a project that highlights the oral
  histories of multidisciplinary artists from Cangrejos (better known as Santurce),
  Puerto Rico. It arose from the lack of an accessible repository that documents and
  celebrates Generation X Cangrejos artists (1964-1981), a group whose cultural and
  artistic legacy has not been widely recognized or preserved. They are a group with
  a broad cultural legacy that we want to recognize as a reference for future generations
  in Cangrejos, in Puerto Rico, in the Caribbean region, and worldwide. Conversations
  with the artists about their work and personal experiences shaped a narrative about
  Cangrejos from each of their disciplines. The oral histories are also condensed
  into interactive digital maps, which establish a dialogue between art, forum, and
  space. It is a collaborative repository in which the community is both author, subject,
  and user, intervening to expand the archive of Cangrejos art as a cultural reference.
  The project is led by La Calle Loíza, Inc., a non-profit organization that since
  2012 has been a cultural beacon on Loíza Street, in the heart of the Machuchal de
  Cangrejos neighborhood, dedicated to creating community cultural projects focused
  on oral preservation and exaltation of artists, spaces, collaborators, struggles,
  and the needs of the community. This Digital Humanities project was conceived by
  participants of the 2024-2025 cohort of the DH Scholars in Residence program of
  the UPR Caribe Digital Project, with the support of the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation
  (https://uprcaribedigital.org/colaboradores/).'

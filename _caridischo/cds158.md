blurb_orig: Few sources survive that record the thoughts and sentiments of the tens
  of thousands of illiterate slaves who carried out the Haitian Revolution. But some
  of that treasure, something of the Haitian revolutionary spirit–its stirring promise,
  and also its tragic disappointments–is still preserved in the books, pamphlets,
  maps, and prints displayed in this room. In many instances the revolutionary spirit
  can be only dimly perceived through the eye-witness accounts and recollections of
  literate white colonists and free people of color, but it is there all the same.
  The exhibition items have been chosen, and the captions written, so as to provide
  a running narrative of the Haitian Revolution.
